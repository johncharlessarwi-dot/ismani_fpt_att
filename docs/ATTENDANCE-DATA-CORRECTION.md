# Attendance data correction workflow

Use this procedure when a staff member or supervisor reports a missing, duplicate, or incorrect attendance record.

## Request intake
- Capture the staff identifier, affected date and shift, correction requested, and reason.
- Do not accept a correction request solely from an unverified third party.
- Ask for the minimum evidence needed to investigate; avoid collecting unrelated personal documents.

## Review
1. Compare the reported record with available check-in events and approved shift schedules.
2. Check device time, timezone, and synchronization status before interpreting timestamps.
3. Identify duplicate events and distinguish device/network failure from deliberate manipulation.
4. Require supervisor approval for manual changes.
5. Preserve the original value and record the corrected value, reason, actor, approver, and timestamp.

## Closure
Notify the requester of the decision and record unresolved discrepancies. Never silently overwrite attendance history or change records to conceal an absence.
